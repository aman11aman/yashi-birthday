For my Yashi ❤️

Happy birthdayyy to my favourite human. 🥹❤️

I really love the person you are.

I want it to be about you.

You mean the world to me. ❤️

Maybe then you'd understand why I think you're so special.

So yeah...

Happy birthday, Yashi. 🥹🎂

Keep eating your food happily, keep making those cute faces, keep chasing your dreams, keep laughing like crazy...

and please keep being you.

Because I love you exactly like that. ❤️
